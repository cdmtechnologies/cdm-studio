Privacy policy

CDM Studio · CDM Technologies, Abuja, Nigeria · Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data CDM Studio collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and, for users outside Nigeria, the main principles of laws such as the EU and UK GDPR.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, a securely hashed password (we never see your password), and two-step sign-in settings if you turn them on.
  • Projects and learning: the apps you build, your lesson progress, exam results, final projects and certificates.
  • Project secrets: API keys you choose to save for your projects, stored encrypted and used only to make the calls you set up.
  • Payments: when you buy credits, Paystack processes the payment. We receive the amount and a reference, never your full card details.
  • Technical data: basic logs such as time, browser type and error messages, kept to run and secure the service.

How we use it

  • To run the AI builder, tutor and marking, save your projects and progress, and issue certificates that anyone can verify.
  • To run your account, take payments, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep the service secure.
  • To answer your support requests.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to run CDM Studio, each bound by their own data protection terms:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage).
  • Anthropic (the AI features: text you ask the AI to work on is sent to Anthropic's API to produce a response; it is not used to train their models under their commercial terms).
  • Paystack (payments).

We may disclose data if the law requires it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Connection tokens for social accounts and API keys are stored encrypted in a secrets vault and are never shown in the app. Access to data is limited by database rules so each person sees only their own workspaces.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account or ask us to, we delete your personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (such as payment records).

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, withdraw consent, or object to processing. See how to delete your data, or email us. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission or your local data protection authority.

Children

CDM Studio is not intended for children under 13, and AI-assisted learning for under-18s should be used with a parent or teacher.

Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
